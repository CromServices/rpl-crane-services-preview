About us and our machinery

A Proline crane and borer, mounted on a Scania

RPL Crane Services has been operating for 14 years. The crane and borer service we provide specialises in removing and installing power poles.

The plant is a 5/10 ton Proline continuous slewing crane, licensed up to 60 ton, with a Proline borer that is excellent for drilling up to 3.6 m. Standard auger sizes are 250–600 mm. Other sizes can be arranged, subject to terrain, soil and rock types.

The crane is always certified and inspected daily. Our crane operators and dogmen are well trained and fully licensed. All insurances and training certificates are current. We can provide a site inspection if required, and a pre-check on site before start can be arranged.
